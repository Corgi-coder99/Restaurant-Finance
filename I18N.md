# Localization

The dashboard, walkthrough, and workspaces support English, Vietnamese, French, and German. The language preference is stored on the device. Number and date formatting follow the locale, while currency follows the selected ledger.

Shared translations live in `dist/i18n-catalog.js` and `scripts/translations.tsv`. Workspace-specific labels live in `dist/workspace/labels.js`. Keep translation keys and placeholders aligned across all languages. Preserve merchant names, addresses, employee names, and other user-entered values.

Run `node test-i18n.cjs` and `node test-pos.cjs` after changing translations.
